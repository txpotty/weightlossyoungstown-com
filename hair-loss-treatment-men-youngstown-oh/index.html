    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in Youngstown, OH

    How Men's Hair Loss Treatment Works in Youngstown

    Male pattern hair loss runs on DHT. DHT is a hormone made from testosterone, and in men who inherited a sensitivity to it, DHT slowly shrinks follicles at the temples and crown until they make thin, short hairs or none at all. Cutting DHT with finasteride protects follicles from further shrinkage. Minoxidil, on its own path, helps follicles grow longer and thicker.

    Who Men's Treatment Is For

    It suits adult men with a receding hairline, thinning crown or diffuse thinning on top. Results are strongest where some hair remains, since long-bald areas seldom regrow. Earlier is better. Sudden, patchy or painful hair loss needs an in-person exam instead. Your photos confirm the pattern.

    Dosing and Daily Schedule in Youngstown

    Finasteride is one 1 mg tablet a day, with or without food. Minoxidil comes as a topical used once or twice a day, or as a low-dose off-label tablet. A compounded combination spray is applied once a day. Dutasteride, when used, is a daily capsule.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Months one and two can bring extra shedding, especially with minoxidil. That shedding usually means older hairs are being pushed out by new growth, and it tends to settle by month three without any change in the plan. Around months three to six, most men see less loss and some thickening. Expect the full effect near month twelve.

    Side Effects of Men's Treatment in Youngstown

    Finasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in roughly one to two percent of men in trials. Most sexual side effects go away after stopping, and some fade on their own. Rarely, men report low mood, which should be reported right away. Topical minoxidil can dry or irritate the scalp. Oral minoxidil can cause extra body hair, ankle swelling or a faster heartbeat.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, heart conditions, blood pressure and every medication you take. Expect questions about mood history, libido and family planning, because each can change the plan. Finasteride lowers PSA results by about half. Tell any doctor who checks your prostate that you take it.

    How Men's Options Compare in Youngstown

    Finasteride plus minoxidil is the standard starting pair for men. When finasteride is not enough, providers may move to dutasteride, which is off-label for hair. Minoxidil with GHK-Cu is a topical-only blend for men who want to skip DHT blockers. Surgery is an option for some.
